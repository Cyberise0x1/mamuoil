# Mamu Oil — Visual Direction

## Three starting directions

### 1. Petroleum Noir
**Very Brief Intro:** A midnight-black energy landscape where warm, molten amber and restrained glass layers evoke refined industrial confidence rather than a conventional filling-station website.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Sunlit Industrial Modernism
**Very Brief Intro:** Pale mineral concrete, warm Nigerian daylight, and editorial typography create a grounded, optimistic energy company presentation.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Heritage Utility Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** An archival-inspired, typographic direction using deep ink, stamped details, and technical cartography to express long-term reliability.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach — Petroleum Noir

### Design Movement
**Cinematic industrial minimalism**, informed by high-end automotive launch films, contemporary editorial art direction, and material-first interface design.

### Core Principles
1. **Material honesty:** Oil should feel viscous, reflective, and physical; metal should feel engineered rather than decorative.
2. **Intentional restraint:** Glassmorphism is reserved for navigation, live-style indicators, and editorial callouts — never used as a generic card treatment.
3. **Directional composition:** Content follows a diagonal barrel-to-flow path, avoiding generic centered landing-page blocks.
4. **Confidence through space:** Large dark fields, sharp information hierarchy, and controlled warm accents create the sense of a premium, established operator.

### Color Philosophy
The palette starts with **crude black** and **iron charcoal** to establish depth and credibility, then uses a proprietary warm **Mamu Amber** to signify value, movement, and energy. Ivory typography is deliberately soft rather than pure white, maintaining a cinematic, composed atmosphere. Translucent smoke-grey glass should behave as an information lens over the dark field, not as the primary visual language.

### Layout Paradigm
The homepage is an **energy current** rather than a stack of modules. The hero occupies an oversized stage, with the barrel visual off-axis and content seated on a vertical editorial rail. Subsequent sections appear as shaped cuts from the main black canvas: a product stream, an operational route, and a contact command panel.

### Signature Elements
1. A rotating, oil-slick 3D barrel form surrounded by drifting motes and a single articulated oil droplet.
2. Thin amber trace-lines that travel across the screen like a measured energy flow.
3. Smoked glass data capsules with delicate inner highlights, used for operational labels and navigation only.

### Interaction Philosophy
Motion should feel **massive and fluid**: elements enter with a weighted glide, the barrel responds slowly to pointer movement, and product tiles become more reflective on hover. Navigation and contact actions remain decisive and fast. Interactions must always preserve a clear path to content.

### Animation
The barrel carries a slow 12–16 second ambient rotation, with pointer tilt capped at a subtle angle. The oil droplet uses an elastic, gravity-led fall and merging effect. Scroll reveals use 500–700ms opacity-and-transform transitions with 50ms stagger timing. Hover states last 160–240ms. All nonessential animation is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System
**DM Serif Display** creates a high-contrast editorial voice for the principal display moments, while **Manrope** provides clean, technically credible body and interface text. Headlines use tight tracking and dramatic scale; utility labels use uppercase Manrope with generous letter spacing; long copy stays calm and readable at a medium weight.

### Brand Essence
**Mamu Oil is a Zaria-rooted energy operator presented with the polish and clarity of a modern African industrial brand.**

Personality: **assured, elemental, precise**.

### Brand Voice
The voice is concise, grounded, and quietly assertive. Headlines should make a declarative point; CTAs should name the next action precisely rather than use generic calls to action.

Example lines:

> Energy, carried with purpose.

> Start the conversation at our Zaria operation.

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a **monolithic M** cut by a single liquid channel. The wordmark pairs a sharp custom serif `MAMU` with a restrained sans-serif `OIL`, suggesting a balance between heritage and engineering. The symbol must work alone as the navigation mark and favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Mamu Amber — #D9852E.** A rich, burnished amber that reads as refined petroleum energy rather than generic gold.

## Style Decisions

- Never use a purple gradient, generic rounded cards, or a centered hero composition.
- Preserve strong light/dark contrast whenever text sits near a visual surface.
- Use glass for active data or navigation overlays only; its presence must communicate information hierarchy.
- Every motion choice should reinforce the feeling of liquid weight, engineered precision, or energy moving forward.
- Copy voice: Mamu Oil speaks as an active Zaria-rooted fuel and oil operator. It never describes the website, a concept, a section, or a future digital presence.
- Layout rule: every major section connects to the next with an amber current, route marker, or numbered operational trace.
- Imagery rule: material macro imagery is balanced with operational context — routes, tanker movement, products, or Zaria/Kaduna signals — so Petroleum Noir remains specifically Mamu Oil.
- Every full-screen scroll segment carries a functional operational artefact: a product signal, route trace, data capsule, capability card, or M-channel mark.
- The hero uses a left editorial rail and right material object, with amber threads visibly pulling the visitor into the supply and operations stages.
